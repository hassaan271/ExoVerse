# Spacecraft models

All files here are unmodified downloads from NASA 3D Resources
(https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources, https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/).
NASA states the assets are free to download and use under its Images and Media
Usage Guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/):
credit NASA, and do not imply endorsement. NASA logos and insignia are not covered.
